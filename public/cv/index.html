02 / THE CREATIVE CV

Sound you can play

From a moving body to a game world: sound as a response to action.

Moventhisizer

Gestural instrument and exhibition installation | UWL | 2024

I designed a body-controlled instrument using iKeleton OSC, Max/MSP and Logic Pro X. Tracking data from 17 body points becomes melodic, percussive and sci-fi sound, with movement controlling pitch, dynamics, filtering and effects.

Binaural sounds follow head position; different limbs have different musical roles. I built adjustable mappings, presets, resets and an iPad control interface through MiraWeb, and reduced mappings when MIDI traffic caused CPU overload. Exhibition visitors explored the instrument through headphones.
